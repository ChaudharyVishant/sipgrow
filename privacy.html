Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. This page explains what happens to your information when you use SIPGrow.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, phone number or email through the calculator. Numbers you type into the calculator stay in your browser.

SIP Tracker data

Entries you save in the SIP Tracker are stored only in your own browser (local storage) on your own device. We cannot see them and they are not sent to our servers. If you clear your browser data or change device, they will be lost.
